# Anti-Slop Checklist

Use these examples to investigate specific usability or content problems. A familiar choice that fits the brief can remain.

- Purple/blue gradients as the only visual interest
- Nested cards for every content block
- Oversized pill buttons with no hierarchy
- Arbitrary bento grids that do not organize content
- Decorative motion that does not communicate state or hierarchy
- Inter (or system-ui) as the only typeface with no role distinction
- Low-contrast gray-on-white body text
- Centered everything with equal weight
- Generic “Get Started” / “Learn More” without product-specific copy
- Shadows and blur used as decoration rather than elevation

Prefer:
- Subject-specific palette and type
- Clear hierarchy and reading path
- Real states (empty, loading, error, success)
- Tokens and consistent spacing
- Motion that supports interaction or narrative
