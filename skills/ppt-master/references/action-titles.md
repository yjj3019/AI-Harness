# Action Titles & Ghost-Deck Test

Load when planning slides.

## Action title rule

Every slide title must be a complete sentence that states the takeaway.

- Bad: "Market Overview"
- Good: "Three channels now drive 80% of growth"

## Ghost-deck test

Read only the slide titles in order.  
They must form a coherent argument a listener can follow without the body content.

If the story is incomplete or jumps, rewrite titles before authoring.

## Typical structures

- Situation → Complication → Resolution
- Problem → Insight → Action
- Claim → Evidence → Implication

Use one structure per deck and keep it consistent.
