# Review Checklist (before claiming done)

## Structure
- Document type (Diátaxis) is clear
- Sections do not mix modes
- Outline matches the reader’s job

## Content
- Every non-obvious claim is sourced or marked unverified
- No invented measurements, costs, or experience
- Interface is documented; implementation is left to code unless needed

## Language
- Plain-language pass completed
- No AI tells remaining
- Terms are consistent

## Fresh reader
- A reader who did not see the drafting can complete the job
- Open questions are listed

## Evidence
- Inside a Phase, record only through the phase gate
- Known limitations and unverified items are listed
