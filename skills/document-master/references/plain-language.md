# Plain Language Pass

Remove or rewrite:

- AI tells: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, empower, unlock, harness (as verb), tapestry, landscape
- Vague intensifiers: very, really, extremely, highly
- Passive voice when active is clearer
- Sentences over 25 words when a shorter version exists
- Jargon without a definition on first use
- Filler openings: "In order to", "It is important to note that"

Prefer:
- Short sentences
- Active voice
- Consistent terms
- Concrete examples
- Reader’s job in the first paragraph
